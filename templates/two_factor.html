How it works

The authenticator code changes every 30 seconds.
  1. Generate a setup secret with your current password.
  2. Add the secret to your authenticator app.
  3. Enter the current 6-digit code.
  4. Future web-panel logins require the authenticator code after the password.
Keep your authenticator backup/recovery method secure. The TOTP secret is never sent to Telegram logs.
